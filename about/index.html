Ayden Taylor

Creative Director @ UOX STUDIO

I'm Ayden Taylor, a creative director from Calgary, Canada, now based in Los Angeles. Most people know me as Math, a nickname I got at 13 because I wasn't very good at math.

I drew constantly as a kid, then taught myself Photoshop, Cinema 4D and After Effects. I started making cover art for artists on Twitter, for free. Robert Gallardo, A$AP Rocky's creative director at the time, found my work and started bringing me in on projects for A$AP Ferg and A$AP Rocky. Those projects turned into real relationships with A$AP Mob.

I dropped out of high school at 15 because I knew exactly what I wanted to do.

In 2017 I went to New York to meet Digital Nas, Steven Victor, Ski Mask the Slump God and Pusha T. On that trip I met Beija Velez, who was at Complex and also working with Usher, and she brought me in on work for Usher. I was making a lot of mixed-media collage then, mostly for Rocky and Ferg, which is what Hypebeast and Complex wrote about in 2018. Collage was one part of it; I was making regular cover art the whole time.

Around 2020 I started UOX Studio with Max Cohen. We worked closely with Young Thug and Juice WRLD, and with a lot of artists from that era, from Lil Tjay and King Von to Yeat and Drake.

In 2024, Daff connected us with Polymarket ahead of the U.S. presidential election. That turned into an ongoing partnership: social and digital content, billboards in New York and Los Angeles, and a takeover of the New York Stock Exchange. UOX has since made work for ESPN, TikTok, Adidas and LØCI.

I've been deep in AI since 2021. Lately I'm building my own models to speed up how I work and to put my taste into tools, and working on web3 projects.
