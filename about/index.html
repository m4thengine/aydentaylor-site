Ayden Taylor

Creative Director @ UOX STUDIO

Ayden Taylor, known as Math, is a Canadian creative director based in Los Angeles and the founder of UOX Studio. He works across cover art, film, 3D and CGI, AI-assisted video, billboards and campaign design for music, sports and brands.

He started making collage art as a teenager in Calgary, creating collage work for A$AP Mob and Usher. His collage work was featured by Hypebeast and Complex. Since then he has made album covers, visuals and merchandise for Drake, Juice WRLD, Yeat, Lil Tjay, King Von and Young Thug.

Through UOX Studio he creates film, 3D and out-of-home campaigns for brands including Polymarket, ESPN, TikTok, Adidas, Under Armour, Bleacher Report and LØCI, including Polymarket's takeover of the New York Stock Exchange and its billboard campaigns in New York and Los Angeles.
