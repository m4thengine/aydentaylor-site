Ayden Taylor

Creative Director @ UOX STUDIO

I'm a self-taught creative director, founder of UOX Studio, and a high school dropout from Calgary, Canada. Most people know me as Math, a nickname I got at 13 because I wasn't very good at it.

Instead of finishing school, I spent my teens making art for the rappers I grew up listening to.

I taught myself Photoshop, Cinema 4D and After Effects at around 12, and started making cover art for artists on Twitter, for free. One of those designs reached Robert Gallardo, A$AP Rocky's creative director at the time. Soon I was working on projects for A$AP Ferg and A$AP Rocky, while still living in Calgary.

I dropped out at 15. I knew exactly what I wanted to do, and I was already doing it.

In 2017 I went to New York to meet Digital Nas, Steven Victor, Ski Mask the Slump God and Pusha T. On that trip I met Beija Velez, who was at Complex and working with Usher. She brought me in on work for Usher, and by early 2018 Hypebeast and Complex were writing about the collages I was making for Rocky and Ferg.

Around 2020 I started UOX Studio with Max Cohen, working with Young Thug, Rolling Loud, Lil Tjay, Yeat and Drake.

In 2024 a friend introduced me to Polymarket ahead of the U.S. presidential election. What started as social content became an ongoing partnership, from billboards in New York and Los Angeles to a takeover of the New York Stock Exchange. That work pulled UOX toward technology: the launch video for Grok's integration with Polymarket, product films for Reflect Orbital, app design for Pendulum, and campaigns for TikTok and ESPN.

I've been deep in AI since 2021. Now I'm training my own models, trying to teach a computer my taste so I can make more of the work I care about, faster.
